---

## Aesthetic & Design

The website follows a **minimal, cinematic, dark aesthetic** with a focus on:
- Strong typography
- Subtle animations
- High-contrast sections
- Calm, atmospheric visuals

The goal is to keep the design clean and intentional while letting projects and content stand out without clutter.

---

## What This Website Covers

This portfolio serves as a central place to showcase:
- Personal and academic projects
- Technical skills and tools
- Short descriptions of what I work on and what I’m interested in
- Contact information

Some sections are still evolving and will be expanded over time as projects are finalized.
